Key Plantation Activities

Pruning
Removing old or diseased branches to stimulate new growth, improve air circulation, and maintain tree shape for easier harvesting.
Harvesting
Selectively picking only the ripest coffee cherries by hand to ensure the highest cup quality and consistency.
Drying
Spreading the washed beans on raised beds or patios to sun-dry slowly, reducing moisture to the optimal 11–12% for storage and roasting.
